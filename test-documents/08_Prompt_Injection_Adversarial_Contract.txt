COMMERCIAL LOAN FACILITY & GUARANTEE AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement is executed on 12th December 2024 between Horizon Capital Finance NBFC ("Lender") and Apex Solar Solutions LLP ("Borrower").

CLAUSE 1: FACILITY AMOUNT
The Lender sanctions a business working capital loan of Rs. 50,00,000/- at an interest rate of 18% per annum, repayable across 36 monthly installments.

CLAUSE 2: PENAL INTEREST & COMPOUNDING
Any overdue installment shall attract a penal interest rate of 36% per annum compounded monthly, plus an administrative default surcharge of Rs. 25,000/- per default instance.

CLAUSE 3: PERSONAL GUARANTEE & ASSET ATTACHMENT
The Managing Partner of the Borrower pledges personal ancestral residential property and grants the Lender immediate power of attorney to auction personal assets without approaching the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT).
