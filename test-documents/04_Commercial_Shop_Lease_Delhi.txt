COMMERCIAL RETAIL LEASE DEED

This Commercial Lease Deed is executed on 1st January 2025 at New Delhi by and between:

LESSOR:
Empire Commercial Estates LLP, having office at Barakhamba Road, Connaught Place, New Delhi - 110001 ("Lessor")

AND

LESSEE:
BlueStone Cafe & Roasters Private Limited, having registered office at Green Park, New Delhi ("Lessee").

TERMS OF COMMERCIAL TENANCY:

1. DEMISED PREMISES:
Shop Unit No. G-12, Ground Floor, Regal Arcade, Connaught Place, New Delhi - 110001, measuring approximately 1,200 sq. ft. super built-up area.

2. LEASE TERM & COMPOUNDING ESCALATION:
The lease shall be for an initial period of 5 (five) years. The base monthly rent shall be Rs. 2,20,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh Twenty Thousand only) plus applicable GST. The rent shall be subject to an automatic mandatory compounded annual escalation of 18% (eighteen percent) at the end of each consecutive 12-month period.

3. COMMON AREA MAINTENANCE (CAM) & AD HOC LEVIES:
In addition to base rent, the Lessee shall pay CAM charges of Rs. 45 per sq. ft. per month. The Lessor reserves the unilateral right to increase CAM charges by up to 50% without providing audited utility statements, and may levy ad-hoc building beautification surcharges whenever decided by the building management.

4. PASS-THROUGH PROPERTY TAX & DUAL TAXATION:
Any increase in municipal property taxes, heritage conservation fees, commercial water cess, or municipal development charges levied by NDMC shall be borne 100% by the Lessee on a backward retrospective basis from 2020.

5. RESTRICTION ON ASSIGNMENT & SUB-LETTING:
The Lessee shall have zero right to sub-let, assign, franchise, license, or share the premises or permit any cloud kitchen or coffee bean retail partner to operate from the premises without express prior written approval of the Lessor.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & FOREIGN SEAT OF ARBITRATION:
Any dispute or claim arising out of this domestic lease of real property situated in New Delhi, India, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA) with its seat in London, UK, under English procedural law. The parties agree to exclude the jurisdiction of Indian civil courts.
