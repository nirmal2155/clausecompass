RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of October 2024 at Bengaluru, Karnataka, by and between:

LESSOR (Landlord):
Mr. R. K. Sharma, residing at #142, Indiranagar 100 Feet Road, Bengaluru - 560038, hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor" (which expression shall include his legal heirs, executors, and assigns).

AND

LESSEE (Tenant):
Mr. Vikram Malhotra, PAN: ABCPM1234F, Aadhaar: 9876-5432-1098, hereinafter referred to as the "Lessee" (which expression shall include his legal representatives).

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of Apartment #402, 4th Floor, Palm Meadows Heights, Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru - 560034 ("Schedule Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. LEASE PERIOD & TENURE:
The lease shall be for an initial period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st October 2024 to 31st August 2025. The Lessee may request renewal 30 days prior to expiry, subject to an automatic mandatory rent increase of 12% (twelve percent) per annum.

2. MONTHLY RENT:
The Lessee agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 45,000/- (Rupees Forty-Five Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via direct bank transfer. If rent is delayed beyond the 5th, a compounding late fee of Rs. 1,000/- per day shall be levied immediately without notice.

3. INTEREST-FREE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Lessee has deposited a sum of Rs. 4,50,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand only), equivalent to 10 (ten) months rent, with the Lessor as interest-free refundable security deposit. The deposit shall be refunded within 90 days after peaceful handover, provided all deductions are finalized.

4. MANDATORY DEDUCTIONS FROM DEPOSIT:
At the time of vacating the Schedule Premises, irrespective of duration of stay or wall conditions, a mandatory non-negotiable deduction of Rs. 45,000/- (equivalent to one month rent) shall be made towards complete repainting and deep chemical sanitization of the premises.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD & EXIT FORFEITURE:
Both parties agree to a strict Lock-in Period of 6 (six) months from the commencement date. If the Lessee vacates or issues notice to vacate during the lock-in period, the entire Security Deposit of Rs. 4,50,000/- shall stand forfeited as liquidated damages, and the Lessee shall further remain liable for rent for the remaining balance of the lock-in period.

6. REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE:
All minor and major repairs including plumbing, electrical fixtures, seepage, water heater maintenance, and structural upkeep shall be the sole financial responsibility of the Lessee. The Lessor shall not be liable for any maintenance expenditure whatsoever.

7. UNILATERAL UTILITY DISCONNECTION:
In the event of delay in rent payment exceeding 15 days, the Lessor reserves the absolute right to enter the premises, lock out the Lessee, disconnect electricity and municipal water connections, and seize all personal movable property of the Lessee inside the premises without approaching any court of law.

8. JURISDICTION & ARBITRATION:
This agreement is governed by the laws of India. In case of any dispute, the Lessee expressly waives all rights to file suit before civil courts or rent tribunals, and agrees that any arbitration shall take place solely before a sole arbitrator nominated exclusively by the Lessor at the Lessor's sole discretion.
