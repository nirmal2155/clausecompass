RESIDENTIAL INTERIOR DESIGN & TURNKEY FIT-OUT CONTRACT

This Turnkey Interior Agreement is made on 5th March 2025 at Hyderabad, Telangana, between:

CLIENT:
Dr. Sumanth Reddy, residing at Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad ("Client")

AND

CONTRACTOR:
DezignCraft Luxury Interiors LLP, having its workshop at Kukatpally, Hyderabad ("Contractor").

SCOPE OF WORK & FINANCIAL TERMS:

1. WORK DESCRIPTION:
Complete turnkey interior woodwork, modular kitchen, electrical lighting, and painting for Client's 3BHK flat at Gachibowli, Hyderabad, for a total agreed estimate of Rs. 14,50,000/- (Rupees Fourteen Lakhs Fifty Thousand only).

2. ADVANCE PAYMENT SCHEDULE:
The Client shall pay the contract sum according to the following stages:
- 20% on design finalization and 3D approval
- 50% upon delivery of raw plywood boards at site (prior to any cutting or assembly)
- 20% upon carcass assembly
- 10% upon final handover
The Client agrees that 90% of the total contract amount must be released before modular kitchen quartz countertops, laminates, and wardrobe shutters are installed.

3. ESTIMATED TIMELINE & NO DELAY PENALTIES:
The estimated completion timeline is 60 (sixty) working days from receipt of the initial advance. However, the Client agrees that this timeline is indicative only. The Contractor shall not be liable for any delay penalties, rental compensation, or damages whatsoever in the event of project delays caused by material supply disruptions, labor shortages, festival holidays, or design iterations.

4. EXCLUSION OF WARRANTY ON HARDWARE & WOODWORK:
The Contractor warrants craftsmanship for a period of only 30 (thirty) days from the date of handover. The Contractor provides zero warranty against termite infestation, water bubbling, laminate peeling, hinge sagging, or hydraulic failure, which shall be deemed normal wear and tear.

5. CANCELLATION & MATERIALS FORFEITURE:
If the Client cancels the project at any stage after the release of the 20% advance, all amounts paid shall be forfeited 100% by the Contractor as design mobilization fees, and no raw materials stored at the site shall be transferred to the Client.
